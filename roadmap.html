Project roadmap

Last updated: Monday 14 September 2026.

Milestone 1: Issue 100 Reached!
As of 15 July 2026, we have successfully scanned and optimised a complete run of issues 1 to 100.

Milestone 2: The Original Run (Issue 184) Complete!
As of Monday 14 September 2026, we have completely rescanned the original story run from issues 1 to 184. Every issue was fully rescanned to including advertisements, print errors, and reprinted stories.

Milestone 3: The Finish Line (Issue 223)
Our next priority is to scan the remaining issues from 185 to 223. We have scanned nine issues so far. Our main contributors have some of these remaining issues and we are receiving assistance from the community for others.

Beyond the Main Run:
Once the core 223 issues are safely archived, we will expand our focus to preserve all special spin-off magazines, holiday specials, and poster magazines.

Want to help or follow updates?

Have the original issues, access to an A3 scanner, and want to help? Find us on the Sonic the Comic the Podcast Discord. All contributions are welcome! You can also follow our Bluesky for project announcements and new rescan updates.
